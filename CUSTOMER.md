## What is Lekha?

Lekha is an AI secretary that lives inside LINE. You add her like a friend and talk to her like one. She remembers things, handles tasks, and connects to your Google account so she can actually help with your real life.

She speaks Thai and English. She uses ค่ะ when you write in Thai. She feels less like a robot and more like the person you wish you had keeping track of everything.

## What she can do for you

**Start your day knowing what's ahead**
- Morning briefing: weather, calendar, tasks, unread Gmail, news — all in one message
- Evening summary: what got done, what's left, tomorrow's prep

**Never forget the important stuff**
- "Remind me to call mom at 6 PM" → done
- "Remind me every weekday at 8 AM to take vitamins" → done
- She warns you 3 hours and 1 hour before something is due

**Stay on top of meetings**
- Pre-meeting alerts: 1 day, 1 hour, 15 minutes before
- Task deadline warnings so nothing slips

**Handle email without opening Gmail**
- "Email Bob the receipt" → she finds Bob, drafts the email, attaches the file, waits for your YES, then sends
- "Summarize my unread emails" → quick bullet list
- "Reply to Bob saying I'll be there" → drafted, ready to send

**Manage your calendar**
- "What's on today?"
- "Schedule lunch with Ana tomorrow at noon" → checks for conflicts, drafts it, creates it on YES
- Full create / update / delete support

**Read and save documents**
- Send a receipt photo → she extracts merchant, total, items, category
- Send a PDF → summarize or extract text
- Send a voice memo → transcribe it

**Search the world for you**
- "What's NVDA at?"
- "Search the web for Bangkok weather tomorrow"
- Real-time answers with sources

**Remember things forever**
- "Remember I prefer aisle seats" → stored
- "Remember my passport expires in March" → stored
- She uses these in future conversations without you reminding her

## How do I use her?

1. Add the LINE Official Account
2. Say "hi"
3. Set your timezone: "set my timezone to Asia/Bangkok"
4. Connect Google if you want: "connect google"
5. Start asking

Everything else is conversation. No special commands to memorize. Just talk to her like a person.

## Is my data private?

Yes. Lekha is private by default. Your data is isolated from other users. Your Google tokens are encrypted. The bot is allowlist-gated, not open to the public.

She doesn't just chat. She acts. Drafts emails, creates calendar events, sends reminders, uploads files.

She doesn't live in a separate app. She's right there in LINE, where you already are.
